About

Dr. Parmita Mondal is a biomedical engineer and researcher building AI‑driven methods to make clinical imaging more robust, interpretable, and actionable. Her work spans computed tomography and angiographic image analysis, with a special emphasis on injection variability correction, projection foreshortening correction, and data fusion to improve aneurysm treatment planning and prognosis.

Research Interests

Quantitative Angiography
Bias reduction, path‑length correction, and precision biomarkers.
Explainable AI
Occlusion prediction with LIME/SHAP; clinician‑friendly insights.
Multimodal Fusion
Alignment and integration of diverse imaging to improve prognosis.
Deconvolution (SVD)
Mitigating injection variability and improving signal fidelity.
Image Reconstruction
Cone‑beam CT; limited‑angle strategies; 3D API.
Clinical Collaboration
Translating algorithms to practical workflows with radiologists.
